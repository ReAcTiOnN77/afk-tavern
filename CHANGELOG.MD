## Version 1.1.0
- Players can now open the tavern for minigames outside of GM-scheduled breaks (opt-in via world setting, off by default)
- New "In the Tavern" / "At the Table" statuses when the tavern is open without a break
- Bulletin Board added to the tavern with a GM-set Message of the Day that persists across sessions
- GM now sees a clean "schedule a break" screen when a player has opened the tavern, with a "View Tavern" button to join if they want
- Custom Asset Paths settings menu: point tavern quotes and word-scramble word lists at your own JSON files without losing your changes on module updates
